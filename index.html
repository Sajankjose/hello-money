What a Money Friend can do

✓ Listen warmly, without judging someone's income or choices
✓ Explain approved, general money concepts with stories
✓ Help people recognise scams and find official resources
✓ Run approved small-group learning activities

What a Money Friend must not do

× Recommend specific financial products or personalised investments
× Collect money, documents, OTPs or account credentials
× Promise profits, claim endorsement or pressure anyone to sign up
× Use the role to generate sales leads or promote a company
